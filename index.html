隐私政策（简体中文）

本政策说明《九九悟空牌》（英文名 Wukong: 81 Ordeals）在 OpenPaaS 海外分发版中如何处理与游戏有关的数据。OpenPaaS/SUD 平台、宿主 App、操作系统和应用商店可能有各自的隐私政策，那些服务不适用本政策。

本游戏是单机游戏，不要求注册账号，不提供云存档、好友关系或微信登录。海外版不收集账号、通讯录或社交关系数据。

1. 我们处理的数据

  • 本机游戏数据：游戏进度、设置、语言选择、解锁记录等保存在你的设备本地。删除游戏或清除应用数据会删除这些本地数据。
  • 可选反馈：如果你主动提交意见反馈，我们会收到反馈分类、反馈文字、本地生成的安装标识、游戏版本、构建渠道和平台标识；如果该版本支持图片且你选择上传，还会收到你选择的图片。请不要在反馈中填写不必要的敏感个人信息。安装标识用于区分同一次安装、限制滥用和处理技术问题，不用于广告追踪。
  • 网络与日志：当你提交反馈或下载游戏资源时，我们的服务器、CDN 和网络服务商可能处理连接所需的网络信息，例如 IP 地址、请求时间、设备/系统/游戏版本、请求结果和错误码，用于提供服务和保障安全。
  • 错误诊断：错误信息默认只保留在设备本地；除非某个版本明确配置了错误上报地址，否则不会上传。
  • 广告：如果本游戏接入 OpenPaaS 流量主广告，广告服务商可能处理设备信息、广告标识和广告交互数据。我们会在启用前更新本政策，并在游戏内提供相应说明。
  • 分发平台：OpenPaaS/SUD 作为分发平台，可能按其自身政策收集设备、安装和运行数据。请参阅 SUD 隐私政策。

2. 处理目的

我们处理上述数据仅用于：提供游戏和保存本机进度；接收并处理你主动提交的反馈；下载和更新游戏资源；保障服务安全、排查故障、限制滥用；履行适用的法律义务。

3. 共享与委托处理

我们不会出售你的个人信息。仅在提供游戏和支持服务所必需的范围内，与 OpenPaaS/SUD（分发和运行平台）、我们的服务器和 CDN 服务商（资源下载、反馈接收和日志记录）共享或委托处理；依法要求时，向司法机关、监管机构或其他有权机关提供。如果后续接入广告、统计或其他第三方 SDK，我们会更新本政策并说明相关数据流。

4. 存储地点与期限

我们的服务器位于中国大陆。海外用户的数据可能传输到中国大陆处理；我们会依据适用法律采取合理措施保护数据。反馈数据在实现处理目的所需的最短时间内保存，之后删除或匿名化；服务器日志按安全运维需要保存，备份中的数据在备份周期结束后按流程清理。

5. 你的选择和权利

你可以不提交反馈；可以通过删除游戏或清除应用数据删除本机数据；可以通过以下邮箱请求访问、更正或删除我们持有的与你有关的反馈数据，或在适用法律允许的范围内撤回同意、提出异议。为保护数据安全，我们可能需要先核实你的身份，并在适用法律要求的期限内处理请求。

6. 儿童

本游戏按 16+ 适龄提示运营，不面向未满 16 周岁的儿童。不同渠道或地区可能要求不同的年龄分级；如果当地法律规定了更高的年龄门槛，以当地法律为准。我们不会在明知的情况下收集儿童个人信息；如发现相关情况，请联系我们删除。

7. 政策更新

如果本政策发生重大变化，我们会更新生效日期，并通过本页面或游戏内说明提供新版本。继续使用游戏表示你已阅读更新后的政策。

Privacy Policy (English)

This policy explains how Wukong: 81 Ordeals (“the Game”) handles data in the OpenPaaS overseas build. The OpenPaaS/SUD platform, host apps, operating systems, and app stores may have their own privacy policies; those services are not covered by this policy.

The Game is a single-player game. It does not require an account and does not provide cloud saves, friend features, or WeChat login. The overseas build does not collect account, contact list, or social graph data.

1. Data we process

  • Local game data: Game progress, settings, language choice, and unlock records are stored locally on your device. Deleting the Game or clearing its app data removes this local data.
  • Optional feedback: If you choose to submit feedback, we receive the feedback category, feedback text, a locally generated installation identifier, the game version, build channel, and platform identifier; if that build supports images and you choose to upload them, we also receive the images you select. Please do not include unnecessary sensitive personal information. The installation identifier is used to distinguish one installation, limit abuse, and troubleshoot issues; it is not used for advertising tracking.
  • Network and logs: When you submit feedback or download game assets, our servers, CDN, and network providers may process connection data such as IP address, request time, device/system/game version, request result, and error codes to provide the service and keep it secure.
  • Error diagnostics: Error information is kept locally on the device by default. It is not uploaded unless a build explicitly configures an error-reporting endpoint.
  • Advertising: If the Game enables OpenPaaS rewarded advertising, ad providers may process device information, advertising identifiers, and ad interaction data. We will update this policy and provide an in-game notice before enabling advertising.
  • Distribution platform: OpenPaaS/SUD, as the distribution platform, may collect device, installation, and runtime data under its own policy. See the SUD privacy policy.

2. Purposes

We process the data described above only to: provide the Game and store local progress; receive and handle feedback you voluntarily submit; download and update game assets; keep the service secure, diagnose failures, and prevent abuse; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information. We share or entrust data only as necessary to provide the Game and support services, with OpenPaaS/SUD (the distribution and runtime platform) and our server and CDN providers (asset delivery, feedback handling, and logging); and with courts, regulators, or other authorities when required by law. If advertising, analytics, or other third-party SDKs are added later, we will update this policy and describe the relevant data flows.

4. Storage location and retention

Our servers are located in mainland China. Data from overseas users may be transferred to mainland China for processing; we will take reasonable measures to protect it in accordance with applicable law. Feedback is retained for the shortest time necessary for the purpose and then deleted or anonymized. Server logs are retained as needed for security and operations; data in backups is cleared in accordance with the backup cycle.

5. Your choices and rights

You may choose not to submit feedback; delete local data by deleting the Game or clearing its app data; contact us at the email below to request access to, correction of, or deletion of feedback data we hold about you; or withdraw consent or object, where applicable law allows. We may need to verify your identity to protect data security, and we will respond within the period required by applicable law.

6. Children

The Game is operated with a 16+ age notice and is not directed to children under 16. Different channels or regions may require different age ratings; where local law sets a higher age threshold, local law applies. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date and provide the new version through this page or an in-game notice. Continued use of the Game means you have read the updated policy.
